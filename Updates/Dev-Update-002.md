# Dev Update #002 — Family House Ground Floor & Grand Staircase Blockout Completed

The Family House has reached a new development milestone: its ground-floor layout and Grand Staircase blockout are now complete in their current blockout form and have been tested in Unreal Engine 5.8.3.

Building on the foundations covered in Dev Update #001, this stage focused on the scale of the house, connections between rooms, and a continuous route from the ground floor to the first-floor landing.

## Family House Ground Floor

The house has progressed from early layout planning into a connected environment blockout with a central grand foyer, side wings, corridors, and a rear section.

The current layout includes living and dining spaces, a kitchen, a library, and service rooms. Room connections and circulation routes have been established, while the foyer has been enlarged to accommodate the main staircase.

The blockout also has clearer material separation and an organized Outliner, making individual rooms, doorways, corridors, and staircase components easier to work with as construction continues.

## Grand Staircase Blockout

The staircase is the main milestone of this update. Its structure uses five ramp segments and four landings to create a multi-stage route through the grand foyer:

- Two lower ramps lead out toward the side landings.
- Two return ramps bring both sides back to a shared central landing.
- One upper central ramp connects that landing to the provisional first-floor landing.

The four landings are the left and right side landings, the shared central landing, and the first-floor landing.

The upper central ramp was shaped into a trapezoidal form, narrower at its lower end and widening toward the top. This establishes the intended silhouette of the staircase while keeping the geometry suitable for blockout testing.

## Gameplay Testing

The complete route was tested in Play in Editor. The player can walk from the ground floor, across the ramps and landings, and onto the first-floor landing without jumping.

This confirms that the current connections and collision setup support the intended walking route. Staircase actors have also been named and organized, and the retained backup ramp has been hidden with collision disabled.

The staircase is still a ramp-based blockout. Individual steps, railings, architectural supports, final materials, and lighting remain future work. The upper landing provides a starting point for the next floor; it does not mean the first-floor layout is already complete.

## Next Steps — First Floor

The next architectural phase is the First Floor and Upper Gallery.

Planned work includes:

- Connecting the upper gallery and floor surfaces to the existing landing.
- Developing the first-floor room and corridor layout.
- Checking ceiling heights, headroom, and how the new floor fits above the ground floor.
- Continuing movement and scale tests as the layout develops.

Contextual traversal is also being considered as a future alternative to unrestricted jumping. It remains a design proposal and has not been implemented.

## Current Development Stage

**Early Development — Level Design & Blockout**

The Family House ground-floor and Grand Staircase blockout milestone is complete. Development now moves toward the First Floor, with detailed environment art and further gameplay integration still ahead.
